System Differentiators

Intelligence that moves material forward

Unlike standard waste apps with decorative charts, EcoSort AI implements closed-loop prediction-driven actions and audit-proof chain of custody.

⚡
Differentiator 0.1

Prediction-Driven Assignment

When delay prediction detects impending traffic or queue bottlenecks (risk ≥ 65%), the system actively re-ranks the collector queue to prevent SLA breaches. High area demand triggers pre-emptive staffing surge alerts before requests pile up.

Differentiator 0.2

Verifiable Per-Item Chain of Custody

Every user item is cryptographically traceable from its initial AI vision scan through scale weighing, facility arrival, and final sorting disposition (recycled kg vs rejected kg). If rejected, the exact reason is shown with verified eco points.
